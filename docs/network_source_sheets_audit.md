# Network source sheets audit

Audit date: 2026-08-14. The five 1536 x 1024 RGBA source sheets below were
inspected at native resolution before the spline-road implementation. Their
original files remain unchanged under `assets/art/sheets/`.

Derivatives are reproducible with
`tools/source_sheet_audit/extract_network_sheets.py`. Every illustrated output
uses a named seed, an explicit source window and alpha-component isolation,
then receives a transparent three-pixel boundary. A post-extraction boundary
check passed for every illustrated component. The four intentionally opaque
road material samples are separately identified below.

## asphalt_sheet.png

Extracted eight topology references under `assets/art/roads/asphalt/reference/`:
`highway_straight`, `town_straight`, `crossroad`, `quarter_curve`,
`t_junction`, `crosswalk`, `parking_bay`, and `culdesac`.

Extracted seven independent props: `guardrail_clean`, three traffic-cone
states, `street_light_clean`, `manhole_clean`, and `storm_drain_clean`.

Two opaque surface samples (`asphalt_surface`, `asphalt_worn_surface`) are
derived from clean pavement pixels for along-spline UVs. They contain no
junction geometry; lane and edge markings are generated by the road profile.

## dirt_road_sheet.png

Extracted twelve references under `assets/art/roads/dirt/reference/`:
`dirt_straight`, `farm_track_straight`, `logging_road_straight`,
`dirt_quarter_curve`, `dirt_s_curve`, `dirt_t_junction`, `dirt_crossroad`,
`dirt_y_junction`, `dirt_turnaround`, `footpath_winding`,
`muddy_logging_road`, and `two_track_road`.

Two opaque material samples (`dirt_surface`, `mud_surface`) provide the base
texture for unmarked spline profiles. The illustrated tiles are references,
not repeatedly stamped road segments.

## waterside_sheet.png

Extracted three water surfaces (`calm_water_tile`, `rough_water_tile`,
`pond_water_tile`), three effects (`foam_patch`, `ripple_patch`,
`rock_outflow`), five bank components (`bank_straight`, `bank_rocky`,
`bank_inlet`, `bank_curve`, `bank_cove`), and four props (`reeds_tall`,
`reeds_short`, `lily_pads`, `shore_rocks`).

Rejected from deterministic extraction: the right-hand waterfall column and
the touching middle-row pond/island compositions. Their alpha silhouettes
touch adjacent artwork, so exporting them would violate the no-neighbour-
remnants requirement.

## railway_sheet.png

Extracted ten network references: straight, curve, switch, T, crossing,
road/rail crossing, overgrown, broken, buffer, and steel rail bridge. Extracted
seven props: ballast pile, sleeper stack, rail signal, crossing signal,
electrical box, utility pole, and chain-link fence.

Railway art is catalogued for future network work; this pass does not silently
turn the road graph into a railway simulation.

## bridge_sheet.png

Extracted all ten isolated bridge compositions under `assets/art/bridges/`:
wide highway concrete, county concrete, green and red steel trusses, stone
arch, timber road bridge, damaged highway, destroyed truss, culvert road, and
timber footbridge.

Bridge artwork remains a placeable object. A placed bridge exposes two derived
road sockets from its county position, scale and rotation; the road authoring
tool snaps endpoints to these sockets. Collision/pathing remains independent
from the visual sprite.

## Runtime use

The spline renderer uses only the four small material samples. Full straight,
curve and junction illustrations remain clean asset-library references; they
are not stamped repeatedly along roads. This avoids the rigid collage effect
while retaining the supplied art as the material and style source.
