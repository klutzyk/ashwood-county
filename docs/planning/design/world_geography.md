# Ashwood County World Geography

The county-scale references in `docs/planning/design/maps/` — **Ashwood Planning map** and **Game world concept map** — are the canonical foundation for Ashwood County's major geography and location relationships. They are conceptual planning references, not literal terrain textures or a 1:1 gameplay coordinate system.

The game world is organized as playable regions within that canonical geography. Each region is a continuous local gameplay space for movement, harvesting, and free building placement; county-scale travel will connect regions later.

The current `World.tscn` is the first rural Ashwood-outskirts region, conceptually west/northwest of Ashwood toward the Farm District and Old Mill Bridge side of the county. Its main access track leads east toward Ashwood and the Highway 16 network, while its smaller northwest track leads toward farms and woodland. Exact region boundaries may evolve, but future regions and major county locations should preserve the relationships established by the two canonical maps.
